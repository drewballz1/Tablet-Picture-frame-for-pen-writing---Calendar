# Bill of materials

Everything here is what the v8r design was drawn around. Substitutes are possible, but several dimensions (marked) come from the exact item.

## Purchased

| Item | Qty | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Lenovo Idea Tab Pro 12.7" (TB373FU) | 1 | Measured dimensions in `MEASUREMENTS.md`. The Tab Pen Plus stylus is used for the pen dock. |
| Heimlove 11 × 17" teak picture frame, with mat | 1 | Rabbet 432 × 279 mm, 12.14 mm deep (measured on one sample; measure yours). The glass and backing board are not used. |
| Statik magnetic pen charger | 1 | Head 8 mm, barrel 10.08 mm (measured). The printed ring tunnel is 11.4 mm wide. |
| Directlinx 2-in-1 USB-C OTG + PD adapter, DL 726-006 | 1 | 38.6 × 16.1 × 8.2 mm. Lets the tablet charge and run the pen charger together. |
| USB-C extension, 240 W, 2 ft, right-angle male to female | 1 | The female end is glued into the ring at the bottom of the frame as the power socket and cable strain relief. |
| USB-C PD wall charger | 1 | Any PD charger the tablet accepts. |
| Black self-adhesive felt, about 1 mm | small sheet | Strips on the liner at the bezel, plus shim stock. The design assumes about 1.0 mm. Measure yours. |
| #4 × 3/4" flat-head wood screws | 8 | Frame to carrier ears. 2 mm pilot holes. |
| Drywall anchors and #6 screws | 5 | Wall mount holes are sized for E-Z Ancor Mini type anchors. |
| Epoxy or CA glue; strong double-sided tape | 1 each | Ring joints, pen charger head into the ring, USB-C socket into the ring, pen cup to the frame side. |
| 7/16" (11 mm) brad-point or Forstner bit | 1 | Pen charger slot in the frame. |
