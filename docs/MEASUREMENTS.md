# Measured values

Taken with calipers unless noted. These are single samples; measure your own tablet and frame before changing anything.

## Frame (Heimlove 11 × 17" teak, landscape)
- Rabbet inside: 432 × 279 mm (tape). Rabbet depth: 12.14 mm. Lip the mat rests on: 6.11 mm wide.
- Flat on the back beside the rabbet: about 9 mm. Frame depth: 25 ± 0.2 mm.
- The frame's own turn tabs sit inside the rabbet and are not used.

## Mat
- 1.01 mm thick. Outside 428 × 276 mm (tape). Opening 293 × 192 mm.

## Tablet (Lenovo Idea Tab Pro 12.7", TB373FU), landscape, screen toward you, camera up
- Body 291.8 (spec) × 189.2 × 6.9 mm, back flat, corner radius about 8.2 mm, bezel about 8.7 mm.
- Front camera: centred, 4 mm from the top. Light sensor: 136.3 mm from the left. The light sensor is in line with the camera centre.
- Top edge: volume 16.4 to 39.6 mm from the left; microphones at 88.5 and 212.8 mm from the left, centred in the thickness.
- Left edge: power 15.3 to 28 mm from the top; speakers 46 to 73 and 118.5 to 145 mm; card tray 92.5 to 112.5 mm. The right edge speakers match.
- USB-C: right edge, about 95.7 mm from the top, toward the back (the opening starts 1 mm from the rear face).
- Bottom edge: keyboard pins and locating slots only.
- Rear camera bump: behind the top-left corner seen from the front; 7.5 to 53.4 mm from the side, 8 to 39 mm from the top, about 2.1 mm tall.

## Pen (Tab Pen Plus)
- Charging port 14 mm from the top end; the taper starts 128.5 mm below the port; the nib tip is 143 mm below it; barrel diameter 9.15 mm; 4.05 mm above the nib.

## Stack, from the wall (mm, model v8r)
- Frame back 6.37, frame face 31.37. Liner face is a 3.0 mm solid white plate. Wall mount is 5 mm thick.
